About CEE Orbital

A bridge between global knowledge and Brazilian audiences.

A significant amount of technical knowledge about cybersecurity, space and technology is available primarily in English. CEE Orbital was created to organize, contextualize and share this knowledge in Portuguese while remaining connected to international sources.

The goal is not to replace original sources. It is to help more people find them, understand them and relate them to the Brazilian context.

Vision and aspirations

Building knowledge that creates curiosity, context and capability.

01 · ACCESS

Make knowledge more accessible

Explain technical concepts clearly while preserving accuracy and identifying the sources used.

02 · CONTEXT

Connect news to real systems

Show how decisions, technologies, incidents and policies relate to risk and operations.

03 · FUTURE

Encourage new paths

Inspire Brazilian students and professionals to explore cyberspace, technology and security.

Technical highlight

SPARTA: exploring threats in space systems.

Space Attack Research and Tactic Analysis (SPARTA) is a public matrix for studying threats, techniques and tactics related to space systems.

CEE Orbital uses SPARTA as one of the references for its Space Cybersecurity, Ground Segment and threat modeling learning track.
